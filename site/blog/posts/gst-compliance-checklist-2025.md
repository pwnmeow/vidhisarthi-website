# GST Compliance Checklist for Businesses in 2025

Staying compliant with GST regulations is critical for businesses of all sizes. Here is a practical checklist to help ensure your business meets its obligations.

## Monthly Compliance

- **GSTR-1** — Outward supply details (due by 11th of the following month)
- **GSTR-3B** — Summary return with tax payment (due by 20th of the following month)
- **ITC Reconciliation** — Match input tax credit with GSTR-2B

## Quarterly Compliance (for QRMP scheme)

- **IFF (Invoice Furnishing Facility)** — Optional for first two months of the quarter
- **GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B** — Due by the end of the month following the quarter

## Annual Compliance

- **GSTR-9** — Annual return
- **GSTR-9C** — Reconciliation statement (if turnover exceeds prescribed threshold)
- **Audit requirements** — Review and reconcile books of accounts

## Key Areas to Watch

1. **E-invoicing thresholds** — Check if your turnover triggers e-invoicing requirements
2. **Input Tax Credit** — Ensure timely reconciliation to avoid disallowance
3. **HSN code reporting** — Mandatory reporting requirements based on turnover
4. **Place of supply** — Correct determination to avoid wrong tax type
