About The Firm

Delivering Reliable & Legally

Established with the objective of delivering dependable legal solutions across advisory, transactional, and litigation matters.

Vidhi Sarthi is a partnership law firm based in Mumbai, Pune, Bhopal, Delhi, and Nagpur. The firm follows a structured approach to legal problem-solving, combining statutory interpretation, judicial precedents, and commercial practicality.

We believe in clear communication, ethical practice, and long-term professional relationships. Our team provides practical and solution-oriented advice with strong drafting and court representation, along with timely and confidential handling of matters on your behalf.
